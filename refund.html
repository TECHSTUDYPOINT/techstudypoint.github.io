Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Thank you for choosing Tech Study Point. Please read our Refund and Cancellation Policy carefully before making a purchase.

1. Digital Products

All products sold on Tech Study Point (PDF notes, study material) are digital products delivered instantly after payment. Since the product is accessed/downloaded immediately, we generally do not offer refunds once the purchase is complete and the material has been delivered.

2. Exceptions

Refunds may be considered only in the following cases:

  • Payment was deducted but the PDF/notes were not delivered due to a technical error.
  • You received a duplicate charge for the same product due to a payment gateway issue.
  • The delivered file is corrupted, unreadable, or significantly different from what was described.

3. How to Request a Refund

If you believe your case falls under the exceptions above, please contact us within 48 hours of purchase via our Contact Us page, along with your payment details/screenshot. Valid requests will be reviewed and refunded within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.

4. Cancellation

Since our products are digital and delivered instantly, orders cannot be cancelled once payment is successfully completed.

5. Contact Us

For any refund or cancellation related queries, please reach out via our Contact Us page.
